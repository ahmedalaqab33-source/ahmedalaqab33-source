# Research development and methods

[Back to profile](README.md)

My long-term research direction is AI-enabled clinical big data science for precision therapeutics and real-world evidence. The topics below describe development priorities; they are not claims of completed projects or independent proficiency.

## Current evidence

The [Clinical Data Analysis Pipeline](https://github.com/ahmedalaqab33-source/clinical-data-analysis-pipeline) demonstrates Python/pandas processing of tabular CSV data, automated quality checks, descriptive summaries, tests, linting, continuous integration and reproducibility documentation.

Tests on controlled fixtures establish software behavior. They do not establish clinical validity, patient-outcome benefit, production readiness or experience with large EHR/claims datasets. Evidence synthesis of AI studies is distinct from training or externally validating models. AI-assisted code requires personal understanding, verification and scientific review.

## Research workflow

Research question → cohort/data specification → quality audit → preprocessing → analysis → validation → clinical interpretation → reproducible outputs → documented repository.

I aim to make assumptions, exclusions, transformations and uncertainty explicit. Data access and publication must respect ethics approvals and applicable permissions. Restricted participant-level data and credentials do not belong in public repositories. Exploratory work is distinguished from confirmatory analysis; results must be traceable to data and executable methods.

## Portfolio priorities

Priorities include automated clinical data quality control, longitudinal medication outcomes, reproducible evidence synthesis, causal inference and carefully validated clinical AI. Scientific validity and clear documentation take precedence over repository count.
